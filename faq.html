Questions about Orbity

Everything you need to know about Orbity, early access, privacy, and how it helps newcomers in Canada.

What is Orbity?

Orbity is an AI-powered platform that helps newcomers settle in Canada with personalized guidance for housing, jobs, banking, healthcare, documents, and daily life.

Who is Orbity for?

Orbity is for international students, working holiday makers, work permit holders, permanent residents, visitors, and people planning to move to Canada.

Is Orbity free?

The waitlist is free. Early users may receive beta access and product updates before public launch.

Does Orbity replace government websites?

No. Orbity helps you understand what to do and where to start, but it is not affiliated with the Government of Canada and does not replace official government information.

Can Orbity help me find a job?

Orbity can help with resume guidance, interview preparation, and career resources. It does not guarantee employment or act as a recruitment agency.

When will Orbity launch?

Orbity is currently in early development. Join the waitlist to receive launch updates and early access invitations.

Is my information safe?

We only ask for basic information to understand user needs and improve Orbity. We do not sell your personal data.
